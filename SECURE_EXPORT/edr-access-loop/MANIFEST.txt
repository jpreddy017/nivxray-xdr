  On this baseline routers/edr_tenancy.edr_tenant() calls
  tenant_registry.authoritative(), which substitutes the literal tenant

    ENFORCE off -> 403 TENANT_NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_PRINCIPAL, requested_tenant "default"

    local fixed -> 403 TENANT_REQUIRED, basis MULTIPLE_AUTHORIZED_TENANTS

  TENANT_REQUIRED variants and deliberately does NOT treat
  TENANT_NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_PRINCIPAL as a selection condition.
