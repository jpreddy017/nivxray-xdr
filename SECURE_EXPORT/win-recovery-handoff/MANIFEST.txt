3 · THE ONE DEFECT, AND THE FIX
--------------------------------
EndpointRecord carries group_id, policy_id, placement_basis, placement_at,
deployment_id, policy_source, policy_assigned_at, policy_assigned_by and
defaults them to None; store.enroll `$set` the whole record, so a
re-enrolment CLEARED them. The enrolment route then saw an endpoint with no
group and assigned the DEFAULT group and policy - silently moving a
recovered computer out of its administrator-chosen group, and replacing a
possibly stricter policy with the default one. The route's own comment
claimed the opposite: its guard was right, but the clearing write happened
one layer below it.

Fix: placement and policy provenance join the delivery record in the
insert-only partition (a new endpoint is still placed at enrolment; an
existing one keeps what it was given), and the placement helpers return the
EFFECTIVE placement rather than the intended one.
